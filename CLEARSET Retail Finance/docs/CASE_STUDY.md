# CLEARSET | A day in retail accounts receivable

## Scenario A: unidentified cash
A bank receipt arrives with an incomplete invoice reference. Use Exception Case Lab to document the investigation, then Reconciliation Detective to apply only a supported amount to the correct customer account. Check the ledger and journal afterward.

## Scenario B: short payment
A customer has paid less than the invoice balance. Confirm whether the difference is an agreed deduction, a credit note, or a disputed amount. Do not invent a credit or write off an amount without evidence.

## Scenario C: period close
Review unapplied receipts, overdue exposure, open disputes and journal entries. Complete the analyst checklist and export the close memo. The controls are a demonstration, not an accounting certification.

## Interview demonstration
1. Open Morning Command and describe the exception queue.
2. Investigate an unapplied receipt and document the next action.
3. Allocate a valid partial payment and explain the cross-customer and over-allocation safeguards.
4. Show Customer 360 and the updated ledger.
5. Export the period-close memo and workpapers.

All customer names, balances, and transactions are fictional. No ERP, Business Central, Rillion, bank or email system is connected.
