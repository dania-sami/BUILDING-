# Ethics, Privacy and Limitations

- All candidate names and metrics are synthetic.
- This demo runs entirely in the browser and persists sample edits in localStorage.
- It does not contact an AI model, ATS, recruitment database, or Knowit service.
- No automated hiring suitability scoring, ranking, rejection or recommendations.
- Drafts and interview materials require human validation before real use.
- Real recruiting use requires privacy assessment, access control, retention rules, accessibility review, security approval and appropriate legal review.
- Cycle-time comparisons in the demo are illustrative and do not establish causal impact.
- The project is independently created and is not a Knowit product or endorsement.
