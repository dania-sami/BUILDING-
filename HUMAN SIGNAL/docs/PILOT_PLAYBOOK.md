# 30-Day Talent Operations Pilot

## Purpose
Explore whether structured role briefs, interview kits, and clearer candidate updates can reduce friction for a technical recruitment team without compromising fairness or candidate experience.

## Week 1: Listen and baseline
Interview recruiters and hiring managers. Map the journey. Define cycle time, feedback and handoff quality consistently. Identify data/privacy constraints.

## Week 2: Co-design
Select one technical role. Draft the role brief and consistent question set. Validate with recruiters and hiring managers. Test accessibility and language.

## Week 3: Facilitate and pilot
Run a 45-minute working session. Use approved templates with human review. Record blockers, adoption and qualitative feedback.

## Week 4: Evaluate
Compare comparable cases; do not assume causation. Check candidate experience, time spent, hiring manager confidence, accessibility, and fairness. Decide whether to iterate, expand or stop.

## Ownership
TA lead: sponsor and process owner. Recruiters: content owners. Hiring managers: technical evidence. Privacy and security: data approval. All hiring decisions remain human.
