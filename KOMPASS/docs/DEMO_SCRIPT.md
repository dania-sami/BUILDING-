# A compelling 5-minute demonstration

1. **Start with ambiguity:** Open Demand intake. Show eight competing fictional requests and the privacy hold on person-level exploration.
2. **Show product thinking:** Open Discovery lab for the processing-time product. Enter the decision it should support, a stakeholder and a testable acceptance criterion. Save it.
3. **Force a tradeoff:** In Portfolio simulator, set capacity to 10, then 25. Explain why high-scoring requests may be deferred and why privacy review cannot be bypassed by increasing impact.
4. **Show reuse:** In Data product studio, inspect the dependency chain from shared quality scorecards to downstream reporting.
5. **Check evidence:** Open Evidence & quality. Point out that fictional regional differences require investigation and cannot establish causes.
6. **Deliver an artifact:** Export the Decision briefing and requirements contract. Show the actual recorded assumptions and governance holds.

**Interview framing:** "I built a small decision-support workflow because the vacancy is about turning business needs into reusable data products, not simply displaying KPIs. I wanted to demonstrate how I would structure the problem, facilitate requirements, surface tradeoffs and keep decisions traceable."

**Integrity:** All datasets are synthetic. This is a portfolio demonstration, not production infrastructure or an agency-endorsed process.
