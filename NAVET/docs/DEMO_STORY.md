# Interview walkthrough

**Scenario:** An AI and Data leadership group needs visibility into its cross-functional work before the next management meeting.

1. **Notice:** The command center identifies a fictional data quality initiative as at risk.
2. **Clarify:** The initiative record shows the accountable owner, progress, and due date.
3. **Coordinate:** Add a follow-up action with a clear owner and deadline.
4. **Document:** Register a pending management decision and the rationale needed for approval.
5. **Facilitate:** Plan a workshop with a specific desired outcome.
6. **Improve:** Rank potential improvements using a transparent heuristic; explain that prioritization is subject to human judgment.
7. **Report:** Download the leadership briefing as a concise meeting pre-read.
8. **Safeguard:** Explain why this prototype intentionally excludes real agency records, personal information, and any actual authorizations.

## Questions to ask the hiring manager

- What recurring coordination work consumes the most time across VO ITI?
- How are decisions, actions and meeting outcomes currently followed up?
- What standards govern safe use of AI and data in management support?
- Which improvement would create value for the team in the first 30 days?
