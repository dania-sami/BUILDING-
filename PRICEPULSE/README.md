# PRICEPULSE ⚡

Hi, I'm Dania. I built PRICEPULSE as a small interactive pricing decision concept inspired by the Pricing Analyst opportunity at Adlibris.

I wanted to explore a simple question: what happens when a pricing decision changes not just sales, but demand, margin, profitability and inventory?

The prototype lets you experiment with prices and demand elasticity, compare campaign discounts, explore clearance decisions, view separate Sweden and Finland scenarios, and export a short pricing decision memo.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it. No installation, accounts or API keys.

## What this is

An independent proof of concept with fictional products and synthetic commercial data. The pricing engine uses an explicit constant elasticity demand assumption. It is intended to demonstrate decision logic, not to claim access to Adlibris data or forecast actual customer behavior.

## What I'd build next

I'd validate the model with real transaction and cost data, add commercial guardrails and controlled experiments, connect pricing and inventory systems, and work with category, purchasing and marketing teams to measure actual results.
