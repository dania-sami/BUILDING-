PRICEPULSE ⚡
Independent concept · Synthetic data

Make every price make sense.

An interactive pricing decision lab inspired by Adlibris. Explore what happens to demand, profit, campaigns and inventory when prices change across Sweden and Finland. No mystery numbers. Every assumption is visible.

Which promotion actually earns its place?

Explore the tradeoff between reducing excess inventory and preserving contribution. Units sold are capped by stock.
