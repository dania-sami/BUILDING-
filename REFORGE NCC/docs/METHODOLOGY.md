# REFORGE methodology and assumptions

## Product design

A material concept is represented by reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP) %, binder %, and the remaining virgin aggregate %. These are illustrative mass fractions, not a physically complete asphalt mix design.

## Unit economics

Indicative cost (SEK/t) = (virgin % / 100 × aggregate cost per tonne) + (RAP % / 100 × RAP cost per tonne) + (binder % / 100 × binder cost per tonne) + transport cost + energy cost.

No labor, capital, plant maintenance, taxes, losses, haul distance, treatment, quality assurance, or risk contingencies are included.

## Environmental proxy

Carbon proxy (kg/t) = virgin percentage × 0.028 + RAP percentage × 0.009 + binder percentage × 0.42 + energy cost × 0.65 + transport cost × 0.22. This intentionally simplified equation uses fictional coefficients and conflates economic inputs with physical activity factors. It is a teaching/demo proxy only, **not a greenhouse gas inventory or LCA**.

Relative proxy saving (%) = (52 - calculated proxy) / 52 × 100. The baseline 52 is synthetic.

## Lab and acceptance

The sample contains synthetic stability, flow, air voids, moisture resistance and rutting indicators. The acceptance screen checks RAP content, unit cost, rutting, moisture and field completeness against fictional customer thresholds. It does not implement any regulatory standard or performance model. Any new formulation requires fresh measured evidence.

## Development gate

The review journal and gate decision record a rationale and accountable team. A green check means only that the **illustrative screen** passed. A real gate would need accredited tests where applicable, compliance review, safety assessment, plant trial, quality controls, customer approval, documented environmental factors and independent expert signoff.
