# Discovery Workshop · 60 minutes

**Goal:** Establish a shared understanding of Sales and Marketing decisions, data sources, and friction.

| Time | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | What decisions are hard today? | Ranked decisions |
| 10–25 min | Walk through lead-to-win process | Shared process map |
| 25–40 min | Compare metric definitions and systems | KPI gaps and owners |
| 40–50 min | Discuss regional differences and privacy | Data constraints |
| 50–60 min | Choose one measurable pilot | Owner, scope, baseline, next meeting |

## Questions I would ask

1. Which recurring leadership decision would improve most with a reliable metric?
2. How do Sales and Marketing define MQL, SQL, opportunity and win?
3. Which systems are sources of truth and where do manual spreadsheets enter?
4. What are the known reporting delays and data-quality risks?
5. Which regions need different processes and which definitions should remain global?
6. What would success look like in 30 days?

## Responsible use

Use aggregated, approved data. Never upload personal CRM data to unapproved tools. Validate every reported conclusion with the accountable owner.
