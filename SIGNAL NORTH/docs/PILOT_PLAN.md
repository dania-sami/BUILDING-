# Four-week Sales & Marketing Intelligence Pilot

## Week 1 · Understand
Interview regional stakeholders, identify top decisions, map sources, agree on pilot scope and success criteria.

## Week 2 · Trust
Document metric definitions, inspect missing fields, deduplicate records, verify currencies and build a quality checklist.

## Week 3 · Explore
Produce funnel and market comparisons, review findings with sales and marketing owners, test two scenarios.

## Week 4 · Decide
Present an executive brief with confidence levels, prioritized actions, and an adoption plan for repeatable reporting.

## Suggested pilot measures

- Percentage of key metrics with signed-off definitions.
- Data completeness for required fields.
- Time to produce the weekly commercial report.
- Stakeholder confidence in reporting, assessed before and after.
- Number of decisions supported by a documented insight.

## Boundaries

Do not infer causal ROI from correlations. Avoid individual employee scoring. All customer and personnel data must remain within approved environments.
