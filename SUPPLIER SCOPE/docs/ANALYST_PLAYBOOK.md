# Supplier intelligence pilot playbook

## Discovery
Interview purchasing, project delivery, legal, sustainability, audit and data owners. Define what counts as a supplier deviation, who validates it, how quickly cases must be assigned, and which data can be shared.

## Data model
Supplier master ID; country; category; purchasing owner; annual spend; delivery KPI; quality KPI; evidence completeness; sustainability indicator; deviation ID; severity; status; responsible owner; opened and closed dates; evidence reference; review timestamp.

## Governance
Separate flags from findings. Document evidence, permissions, provenance and review dates. Never infer legal noncompliance from a missing field. Human review is mandatory for high-stakes decisions.

## Suggested first 30 days
Week 1: stakeholder discovery, process mapping and KPI definitions.
Week 2: source inventory, entity resolution and quality baseline.
Week 3: pilot dashboard and deviation triage with two purchasing teams.
Week 4: measure adoption, revise controls and prepare scale-up recommendation.

## Pilot metrics
Median deviation triage time; percent of records with validated IDs; evidence completeness; closure timeliness; stakeholder satisfaction. Baselines and outcomes must be measured rather than assumed.
