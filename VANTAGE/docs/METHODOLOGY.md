# Financial methodology and controls

## Units and scope
All monetary figures are fictional SEK millions. Six illustrative Nordic businesses; Q3 2026 reporting period. Revenue quarterly profiles are fabricated and sum approximately to annual totals.

## Formula definitions
- EBITDA margin (%) = EBITDA / revenue × 100.
- EBITDA growth (%) = (current EBITDA / prior EBITDA − 1) × 100.
- Cash conversion (%) = free cash flow / EBITDA × 100. This is an illustrative proxy, not a standardized cash conversion definition.
- Net leverage (x) = net debt / EBITDA.
- Enterprise value = EBITDA × selected EV/EBITDA multiple.
- Illustrative equity value = enterprise value − net debt.
- Scenario equity = EBITDA × (1 + EBITDA scenario %) × (base multiple + multiple adjustment) − (net debt + debt adjustment).

## Exception thresholds
- Cash conversion below 70%: challenge working capital and earnings quality.
- Net leverage above 3.5x: review covenant headroom and refinancing.
- Reported revenue differs from synthetic ledger: reconciliation exception.
- ESG data coverage below 85%: incomplete reporting.
- Negative EBITDA growth: request explanation.

Thresholds are educational examples, not Norvestor policy. Each exception requires source validation, contextual assessment and analyst judgment.

## Exclusions
This is not a fund accounting system. It does not model ownership percentages, fund NAV, currency translation, valuation guidelines, IRR, MOIC, LP cash flows, fees, tax, carried interest, commitments, or regulatory reporting. No real market data or ESG disclosures are used.
